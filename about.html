About Me

I’m Amin Mansuri, a Finance professional with 15+ years of experience across Finance Operations, Accounts Payable, Reporting, Compliance, ERP implementation, and business process improvement.

Over the years, I’ve worked at the intersection of Finance and Technology — not just managing financial processes, but continuously looking for ways to make them faster, smarter, more accurate, and less dependent on manual work.

My core strength is understanding a finance process from the business and accounting perspective first, and then using technology to automate and improve it.

I work with tools and technologies including Python, Excel, Power BI, SQL, Oracle NetSuite, SuiteScript, APIs, databases, and workflow automation to build practical solutions for real finance problems.

What I Do

I focus on building finance automation solutions around areas such as:

  • Finance & Accounting Automation
  • Accounts Payable & Accounts Receivable Automation
  • Invoice and Vendor Processing
  • Bank Reconciliation & Transaction Matching
  • Financial Reporting & MIS Automation
  • Data Warehousing & Finance Dashboards
  • Revenue & Rebate Automation
  • ERP & NetSuite Automation
  • Excel / Python-based Finance Tools
  • Process Improvement & Control Automation

I believe automation should not simply replace a manual task. A good automation should improve the entire process — reducing errors, strengthening controls, saving time, improving visibility, and enabling finance teams to focus on higher-value work.

My Approach

My approach is simple:

Understand the process → Identify the problem → Analyse the data → Design the solution → Automate → Measure the impact.

Having worked on finance processes from the ground level as well as ERP and automation projects, I understand the practical challenges that businesses face — messy data, repetitive Excel work, manual reconciliations, disconnected systems, reporting delays, and processes that depend too heavily on individuals.

That experience helps me build solutions that are practical, finance-friendly, and designed for real-world business environments.

Finance Meets Technology

My long-term focus is to help finance teams move from:

  • Manual → Automated
  • Excel-heavy → Data-driven
  • Reactive → Proactive
  • Transaction-focused → Insight-driven

I’m particularly interested in the emerging role of technology in Finance — including AI, automation, analytics, ERP integrations, and intelligent financial workflows.

I build solutions with one objective:

Make Finance work smarter.
